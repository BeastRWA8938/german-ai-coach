
# ProjektDeutsch AI

## AI-Powered German Practice Coach

Version: 1.0

Author: Rushikesh

---

# 1. Problem Statement

German learners often need topic-specific practice exercises for levels such as A1, A2, B1, and B2.

Currently, learners must:

* Search multiple websites
* Find exercises manually
* Verify answers themselves
* Keep track of weaknesses manually
* Revisit previously studied topics without guidance

This process is fragmented and inefficient.

The goal of ProjektDeutsch AI is to provide unlimited, structured, topic-based German practice exercises powered by the user's preferred LLM provider while tracking learning progress and identifying weak areas.

---

# 2. Vision

Create an AI-powered German Practice Coach that:

* Generates unlimited grammar exercises
* Evaluates answers instantly
* Explains mistakes
* Tracks topic mastery
* Detects weaknesses
* Encourages spaced revision
* Works without requiring the platform owner to pay AI inference costs

---

# 3. Target Audience

Primary Audience:

# 8. Confidence Decay System

Purpose:

Encourage revision without punishing users.

---

## Mastery

Represents historical performance.

Does not decay rapidly.

Example:

Dativ Mastery

90%

---

## Confidence

Represents certainty that knowledge is retained.

Decays over time.

Example:

Day 1

Confidence: 100%

Day 14

Confidence: 85%

Day 30

Confidence: 70%

Day 60

Confidence: 50%

---

## Formula

Topic Score

= Accuracy × Confidence

Example:

Accuracy = 90

Confidence = 50

Topic Score = 45

Requirements:

* Real-world German
* Grammatically correct
* Appropriate CEFR level
* Fill-in-the-blank format
* Include accepted answers
* Include grammar rule
* Include explanation

Return only JSON.
